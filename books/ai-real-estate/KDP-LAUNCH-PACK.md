```html
<p>المشكلة مش إنك مش شاطر. المشكلة إن <b>٨٠٪ من وقتك بيروح في شغل متكرر مش محتاجك</b>.</p>
<p>الكتاب ده مش كلام نظري عن الذكاء الاصطناعي. ده <b>نظام شغل</b> مبني على ٨ خطوات، كل خطوة فيها:</p>
```

### Book description (HTML)

```html
<h2>If your day is 37 WhatsApp messages and zero closings — this is for you</h2>
<p>You're an agent. Your day: 37 messages, two viewings, a client saying "too expensive", and by evening you can't remember what you actually did.</p>
<p>The problem isn't that you're bad at this. The problem is that <b>80% of your time goes into repeat work that doesn't need you</b>.</p>
<p>This is not a theory book about AI. It's a <b>working system</b> built on eight steps. Every step gives you:</p>
<ul>
<li>The real problem (the one nobody names)</li>
<li>The method that fixes it</li>
<li>A ready prompt you can use immediately</li>
<li>A before / after example</li>
<li>A 10-minute exercise</li>
</ul>
<h2>What you'll actually learn</h2>
<ul>
<li>How to split your work into three baskets — and what to delete</li>
<li>A listing prompt that returns three headlines plus the three questions buyers ask</li>
<li><b>How to answer "the price is too high" without cutting a dollar</b> — by separating three different objections</li>
<li>How to handle "it's far" and "I don't feel comfortable" with minutes and experience, not arguments</li>
<li>A four-column follow-up sheet and a 2–7–21 cadence that revives quiet deals</li>

<p>Four practical appendices: 12 ready WhatsApp messages, 12 FAQs, a 30-day tracker, and a plain glossary — with five diagrams.</p>
```
