## Appendix B: The five-minute list (emergencies)

When you simply can't start, pick anything here — each takes five minutes or less, and each is real work:

1. Clear five old emails.
2. Create a folder on the desk and name it properly.
3. Write the first sentence — even a bad one.
4. Fill a glass of water.
5. Remove three things from the desk.
6. Send one message you've been postponing.
7. Write only the section headings, no content.
8. Check one appointment in the calendar.
9. Delete twenty photos from your phone.
10. Write three things you'll do tomorrow.
11. Open the file you'll work on tomorrow and leave it open.
12. Close five tabs.
13. Write one sentence about what stopped you.
14. Make one short call.
15. Tidy the charger cable.
16. Write the task name on a sheet and put it on the desk.
17. Read one page of your reference.
18. Write one thing you finished today.
19. Set a two-minute timer and just sit.
20. Drink water and look out the window for three minutes.

## Appendix D: Twelve sentences to tell yourself

Not slogans. Short lines, said at the right moment:

1. "I don't need to be ready. I need to start."
2. "Two minutes beats zero."
3. "This is a big task, but the first gravel is small."
4. "I'm not lazy, I'm unclear."
5. "I can do 80% and ship it."
6. "Today is a mud day, so it's mechanical work."
7. "I won't answer now — I'll get back to you at such a time."
8. "One missed day isn't a disaster; two are."
9. "I don't need to finish. I need to begin."
10. "What's in my head isn't a plan — write it."
11. "Poor work can be fixed; zero doesn't exist."
12. "I don't need to understand everything to take the next step."

## Appendix E: Quick glossary

| Term | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **The silly start** | The first two minutes of a task, in any form, with no commitment to continue |
| **Gravel** | A 10–15 minute step, physical and photographable |
| **Rock** | A stage of the task, made of several pieces of gravel |
| **Mountain** | The big task as it appears before breaking down |
| **Sharp hour** | The time of day your mind runs at its best |
| **Mud hour** | The time of day focus is nearly impossible |
| **Wave** | A 45–50 minute session plus 10 minutes off |
| **Friction** | The seconds and steps between you and something |
| **Inbox** | The single place where anything new gets written |
| **Transition card** | Three lines you write before stepping away |
| **List of three** | Important + quick + backup, nothing more |
| **Clean stop** | Leaving work in a way that makes returning easy |
