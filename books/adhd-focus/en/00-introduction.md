# Introduction: You're Not Lazy, and You're Not Broken

## Before we start

There is one thing people say to anyone who struggles to focus, and they say it kindly: "you just need to organise your time."

That sentence is wrong. Because time management assumes a fixed supply of attention that just needs a schedule wrapped around it. In reality your focus isn't steady — it's **intermittent**, and it moves with light, sound, how many tabs are open in front of you, your mood, caffeine, and how you slept.

That is why the planners fail. Not because you're weak. Because the planner was designed for a different brain.

## What this book is and isn't

**It is:** a practical system for people whose work demands focus and whose brain doesn't always cooperate. Small steps, cheap tools, and methods that survive after the motivation dies.

**It is not:** a diagnosis, a treatment, or a substitute for a doctor. If you have ADHD symptoms or any health condition, a professional belongs in the picture, and this book is a **supplement** to that work, never a replacement.

Nor does this book claim ADHD is a gift, or that it isn't a real difficulty. It simply deals with the reality as it is: some brains chase fast rewards, get bored by long tasks, and forget what they were doing an hour ago. This book builds a system around that fact instead of trying to break it.

## A small promise

The chapters ahead will not tell you to "be organised". Each one gives you one thing you can try today — and if it doesn't work, tell me and we'll adjust it together.

Every chapter has: a problem, a method, a ready tool, and one rule to keep.
