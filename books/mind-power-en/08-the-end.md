# The End: A Last Message... and the First 21-Day Plan

## The First 21-Day Plan

The idea is simple: 21 days = 3 weeks. Each week is a round through the rules of the book... but the tool changes:

- **Week One: See** — you try every tool once.
- **Week Two: Anchor** — the tools turn into a small daily routine.
- **Week Three: Integrate** — the tools have become life.

### Week One — See (Days 1-7)

The rule of the week: one day = one tool, once. No mastery, no ambition. The only goal is that you see each tool's face:

- **Day 1 — The Watcher:** a 5-minute session. Paper and pen: "I noticed a thought saying: ..." no judgment. Five thoughts.
- **Day 2 — The Bill:** open your screen time and write the number on paper. Do the yearly calculation. And lock the notifications that are not necessary.
- **Day 3 — The Radio:** all day long, log: "I heard myself saying: ..." and at the end of the day, pinpoint the ruling sentence.
- **Day 4 — The Edit:** write the replacement for your ruling sentence — a sentence of honesty. Fix it in front of you and make it your phone wallpaper. And every time you hear the old one, say the new one.
- **Day 5 — Deliberate Repetition:** a 5-minute session: the sentence ten times in a low voice, and one real image of something you did well, three times.
- **Day 6 — The One Minute:** pick your postponed thing, write its minute version, and execute it immediately. If you stop after the minute — you still won.
- **Day 7 — The Chargers:** two lists: five chargers and five drainers. And choose only three decisions for the coming week (sleep, movement, people or screen).

### Week Two — Anchor (Days 8-14)

The small daily routine — all of it together no more than a quarter of an hour:

- **Morning (about ten minutes):** deliberate repetition, 5 minutes + the one minute on your postponed thing + waking up at the same time.
- **Evening (five minutes):** the Watcher + the energy question: how many out of ten? + today's screen time number.
- **Throughout the day:** edit the sentence every time you hear the old one... and continue the three decisions you chose on day 7.
- **Day 14 — mid-plan review:** a quarter of an hour with yourself: what is working? What stopped? Adjust, but do not close down anything that is working.

### Week Three — Integrate (Days 15-21)

- Continue week two's routine exactly as it is — **without any additions**. The one minute became half an hour? Normal. The new sentence arrives before the old one? That is the success now.
- **Day 18:** choose one decision only to become fixed for the whole coming month — this is the seed of your new routine.
- **Day 21 — the big day:** write yourself a small message: "Today, after 21 days..." and what changed. And write the first line of the sixth story: "A year from now, I..."

One last note about the plan: this plan will not be completed perfectly. There will be lost days — and that is not failure, it is data. The day that gets lost, the day after it says: return with the smallest step... and continue the count. The one who continues after falling... is the one who truly continues.
