## ٢) English Edition

### Title
The ADHD Focus System

### Subtitle
How to Start and Keep Going When Your Brain Won't Settle — 8 Chapters and a 30-Day Plan

### Description

```
<b>You're not lazy. You're not broken.</b><br><br>
If you can sit in front of a task for an hour and still not start it, if your plans die on day three, if your brain treats "later" like a promise — the problem isn't willpower. Your brain runs on a different reward system, and this book builds a system that works <i>with</i> it instead of against it.<br><br>
<h4>What's inside</h4>
8 chapters and 5 printable appendices, built on one idea: <b>don't fix your brain — fix what's around it.</b><br><br>
<ul>
<li><b>The start card</b> — three lines that turn "I can't begin" into a ready decision.</li>
<li><b>The mountain map</b> — breaking any big task into gravel you can touch in 10 minutes.</li>
<li><b>The twenty-second rule</b> — less friction on the good, more on the bad.</li>
<li><b>Three environment layers</b> — physical, digital, social; one isn't enough.</li>
<li><b>Your mental day</b> — when to work and when to let it go, guilt-free.</li>
<li><b>A system outside your head</b> — one inbox, one review, three tasks.</li>
<li><b>Boundaries</b> — one refusal sentence that protects your time without a lecture.</li>
<li><b>A 30-day plan</b> — one foundation per week, 15 minutes a day.</li>
</ul>
<h4>Who it's for</h4>
Anyone who struggles with starting and continuing: students, employees, founders, parents running a household. You don't need a diagnosis to benefit — and you don't need one to struggle.<br><br>
<h4>An honest note</h4>
This book is <b>not a diagnostic tool and not medical or psychological advice</b>, and not a substitute for a qualified professional. Any diagnostic or medication decision belongs with a doctor. What you'll find here: systems, habits, and self-organisation tools you adapt to your own life.<br><br>
<b>9 diagrams · printable appendices · 79 pages · start from any chapter</b><br><br>
From the <b>Mind Power Series</b> — short, practical books without filler.
```
